# Strategic Context: India-Japan AI Strategic Dialogue (2026)

- **Framework:** Japan-India AI Cooperation Initiative (JAI)
- **Event:** Inaugural Strategic Dialogue held in Mumbai (April 21, 2026)
- **Mandate:** Promoting co-creation, policy convergence, and trustworthy AI governance ecosystems.

### Relevance to Aegis-VM
The bilateral summit emphasized the cooperative development of robust, localized, and innovative AI tools. Cloud computing infrastructure is currently dominated by expensive, centralized providers. Aegis-VM supports the JAI mandate by delivering a local, zero-cost, lightweight software sandbox. This allows developers in both nations to safely run and evaluate autonomous systems directly on local terminals or edge devices without risking host-level security or depending on external cloud monopolies.
